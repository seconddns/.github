# SecondDNS

Managed secondary DNS service — reliable slave DNS hosting for your zones.

## What we do

SecondDNS provides secondary (slave) DNS hosting: your primary nameserver
stays in control, we mirror your zones across a network of geographically
distributed nameservers and keep them answering even when your primary is
down. Zone transfers (AXFR/IXFR), NOTIFY support, DNSSEC-friendly, with
hosting panel integrations (Plesk, DirectAdmin, CyberPanel) and monitoring
templates (Nagios/Icinga, Zabbix).
